Battery research findings

A laboratory prototype retained 92% of its battery capacity after 1,000 charging cycles. The experiment used 24 cells under controlled temperatures.

Limitations

The study did not evaluate mass production or extreme weather. Independent replication is needed before commercial claims can be made.
